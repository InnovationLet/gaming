InnovationLet Gaming is the games desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the studios that make games, the platforms that sell them, and the economics that decide which projects survive. No review scores, no hype cycles. Just how games get funded, built, and shipped, and what that means for the people playing them.

## What we cover

- **Studios**: developers and publishers, project scale, and the layoffs and closures behind the numbers.
- **Platforms**: consoles, PC storefronts, mobile, and the revenue splits that shape them.
- **Engines**: the tools and technology behind modern games, and the trade-offs teams accept.
- **Esports**: leagues, sponsorship, and the business models keeping competitions alive.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: financial disclosures, developer statements, and storefront data. When we cite a claim, we link it. When a company won't confirm a budget, we say so. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does InnovationLet Gaming cover?**
Studios, platforms, engines, and esports. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.

**How often is it updated?**
The desk publishes daily during the week. Subscribe to the newsletter for a weekly round-up.

**Can I pitch a guest post?**
Yes. Send a short pitch to hello@innovationlet.com. We review submissions weekly and reply to every one.

**Where can I find the other categories?**
